**Design principle, agreed with the owner:** tell a child what they *can* do, not what they
must do. Every screen has one invited action (it pulses, the narrator names it first, an idle
nudge repeats it) and the other options visible and named once. Nothing a child taps is a

first flight has happened.

**Observed on the real tablet, one child, one session.** They liked the game. Three problems:

   sequence that opened it. Look at `setJournalOpen` and the About handler in `src/ui/ui.ts`,

   grown-ups panel.
2. **Turning the world to reach the hidden last place was frustrating.** The hunt hides one

   swipe needs so one short swipe turns further. Keep the drag working. The hand coach in
