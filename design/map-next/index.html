Space map: what's next, shown

Playtest 2: back on the Space map after a world, the child didn't know what to do. "Mars is ready" was spoken and the "Tap Mars" pill was written, and neither reached a child who can't read yet. These captures show the map at each step of a fresh adventure, from main (before) and this branch (after).

✓ tick: you've been there ⬇ bouncing arrow: go here next (same crayon as the hunt arrow) ring in the sky: the same world, pulsing; the parked ship's nose points at it too

1 · First map

The arrow sits on Earth from the very first screen, and the ring now circles Earth in the sky. Before, the ship aimed at the Moon whenever Earth was suggested; now its nose points at Earth.

2 · Tapping a locked world

Saturn tapped on a first-run map. Before: "Visit Mars first", pointing at another padlock. After: the arrow and the ring jump to the Moon (the nearest world a child can actually go to), its card bounces with a sun outline for about 2.6 s, then everything settles back. "The" mid-sentence is now lower case.

3 · Back from Earth

Earth wears a green tick; the arrow and the ring move to the Moon.

4 · Back from the Moon: Mars unlocks

Held at 0.4 s into the unlock moment. The Mars card pops, its padlock flies off with a sun sparkle, and at about 0.9 s the arrow drops onto it. Before, the card just turned gold.

5 · Back from the Moon, settled

Two ticks, the arrow bouncing on Mars, Mars ringed in the sky.

6 · Back from Mars: Saturn unlocks

Three ticks; the same unlock moment, then the arrow on Saturn. Saturn's ring is now about a fifth smaller than in the first review (owner: "make Saturn's ring smaller on tablet"). The ring's size is in world units, so it is smaller on the phone too.

Notes

  • The "Tap X" pill stays as a reinforcement; it is no longer the only cue.
  • Reduced motion: no bounce, pulse, pop or sparkle. The arrow and ring stay, still.
  • "Done" means visited, the same fact that unlocks the next world, so ticks and padlocks always agree.
  • The live map shows every world on one screen, so there is no page to turn to. The neighbourhood pager in src/map/ isn't wired in yet.
  • New crayon use: grass for the done tick, sun for the map arrow and the unlock sparkle. Both added to the colour table in AGENTS.md.
