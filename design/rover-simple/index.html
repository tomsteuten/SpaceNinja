Mars rover, one verb

After playtest 2: arrows on a grid, moves acting seconds later, a queue of eight and exact parking for Drill were too much. Now the child does one thing: tap a rock.

  • Rocks still to drill glow gold (like the places in the world) and wobble gently. Each has a big invisible tap target (at least 64 px).
  • A glowing dot flies from Earth to the rover in 2 s. The clock's minute hand sweeps 12 minutes, the real wait on a typical day, and the face fills yellow behind it. It stays filled while the rover works.
  • Only when the dot lands does the rover drive itself to the rock and drill. A tube fills; three tubes bring new rocks.
  • Taps while busy get a soft shimmer ring round the rover and are forgotten, not queued.
  • Caption: "Tap a rock!", "Mars is far away!", "A rock sample!", "Three samples!", then back to "Tap a rock!". No "Wait…".

About these captures

Captured with reduced motion on (as the review script always does), so the rocks don't wobble here and the dot cuts in thirds instead of gliding. "Before" is today's main, driven to the same moments with the arrows.

Proposed narration, not recorded: "Tap a rock. Your message takes a long time to reach Mars!"
