Done, made visible

Fix 5 from the state-change audit. "Done" comes at three sizes: a visit (three places), a world (all six) and the whole game. None of them shows well today. A finished visit opens a journal that is half dashed "Still to find" tiles, and once every world is visited the map sends the child back to a finished world with the same gold card a brand-new world gets.

These are design mocks: the real build, captured at each state, then the same screen with a mock stylesheet and script laid over it (design/done-visible/mock.css, mock.js). Nothing in the game has changed yet.

3 found! trip sticker: this visit is finished ✓ tick: found / been there (the map's tick) ★ sun star: a whole world found ●●●○○○ pips: places found and left, the journal's own track

A1 · Journal after the first finished visit (Earth)

Before: three photos mixed in among three dashed "Still to find" tiles, in the book's order, so the Night Side sits last behind two empties. After: the visit's three snapshots lead the page, tilted like the postcards, each with a green tick, under a big sun "3 found!" sticker. The three places left become a small calm "Next time" strip with a rocket and three gold-place rings, the same ring the child hunts on the world. The world chips move below.

A2 · Journal mid-game (Mars, with Earth complete)

The same page after a Mars visit, with all of Earth and half the Moon already found. Earth's chip swaps its "6/6" for a sun star: a whole world done. Mars leads with its trip.

B · Space map once every world is visited

Before: the Moon card is gold with the arrow, exactly like a world that just unlocked. After: every visited card carries its journal track as pips (sun = found, white = left). The suggested Moon keeps the gold, the tick and the bouncing arrow, and its half-white pips are the picture of "more to find here". Earth, fully found, wears a sun star in place of its tick. The pill's journal icon becomes the gold-place ring; the words stay as a reinforcement.

For comparison, a brand-new unlock as approved in map-next: no tick and no pips, a padlock that pops off with a sparkle, then the arrow.

Notes for building it

  • The trip layout appears only when the journal opens on a visit whose three places are all found. Opened any other time (from the map, mid-hunt) the page keeps found photos first and the "Next time" strip in place of dashed tiles, without the sticker.
  • "This trip" is the visit's own three finds (the mission already knows them), not the last three in storage.
  • A world with all six found shows its six photos and no strip; the sun star replaces the count on its chip and the tick on its map card.
  • Crayons: sun for the trip sticker, the star and found pips; grass for the photo ticks (the same "done" tick as the map). Both added to the AGENTS.md table on implementation.
  • Owner review: dots bigger on phone. They are now 11 px (10 px on tablet) with a 26 px world picture, so the phone card keeps its 76 px height.
  • Reduced motion: nothing here moves except the arrow, which already follows the reduced-motion rule.
  • Not in scope: the finale (whole game done, audit state 24) is unchanged.
