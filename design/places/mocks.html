Moon visit, on arrival Hunt gone: no gold rings, no counter, no Journal. A third card, Places, joins Jump and Moonlight on the shelf.

The same on every option below. The ship that parks over the Moon in portrait is left out here; it is a separate leftover.

Places · Option A Pinned photo: the photo hangs beside the globe on a string to the spot. Tap Places again for the next place.

Next: the Places card itself; while a photo is up it wears a green arrow. After the sixth place it starts again. Put away: spin the Moon or tap anywhere off the photo. The photo is small enough that the Moon stays the main thing.

Places · Option B Big photo: the photo takes the top of the screen, the Moon shrinks below it. A big arrow on the photo goes to the next place.

Next: the big green arrow sits on the photo, where the eye already is; the Places card does the same. Put away: spin the Moon or tap the space around it; the Moon grows back. The photo is the star; the Moon is smaller while it is up.

Places · Option C Photo strip: Places lays out all the photos in a row above the shelf. Tap any photo; the Moon turns to it.

Next: the child picks by picture, in any order; tapping Places again also steps along. Put away: spin the Moon or tap Places once more; the strip folds away. Shows every place at once, so it is the busiest of the three.
