Space Ninja · state-change audit · after playtest 2

What just happened, and what now?

Every state change a child meets, in order, checked against the owner’s rule: a child must be able to tell what just happened and what they can do next, from the screen rather than the voice. Shots are full-resolution captures of the playtest build (e2e/state-audit.pw.ts), phone 390×844 and tablet 768×1024, motion on. Each state was checked against the code (src/main.ts, src/ui/ui.ts, src/toys/, the CSS). Observations only; the coordinator ranks fixes.

Branch claude/state-audit-115112 · 2026-10-22

The five biggest confusions

  1. Back on the Space map, nothing shows what just happened or what is new.After a visit the only change is which card is gold. The world just finished gets no tick or sticker, the next world does not move, glow or get pointed at, and a world unlocked this moment looks exactly like an old suggestion: the code sets is-new on its card but no CSS styles it. Gold is never explained, so it reads as “this button is pressed”. “Mars is ready” and “Tap Mars” are words in a pill and a voice.
  2. After the third find, the ring and the voice point different ways.The paper ring moves toy → Journal → Space map, and the Space map ring only appears once the journal has been opened. Meanwhile the voice says “You can fly home and pick another world” (Earth, no toy). On a first Earth visit the bedtime spin rings Space map before any gold place is found, so the ring says “leave” while the gold places say “find”.
  3. The Mars rover asks too much of a 5-year-old.Four arrows on a perspective grid (is “up” away from me?), moves that act three seconds later, a queue of up to eight, and Drill that only works when parked exactly on a rock. Drilling plain ground does nothing visible, and the caption stays on “Wait… Mars is far away!” after everything has landed. The idea (“12 minutes”) is only text.
  4. “Done” means three different things and none is shown on the map.A visit is done at 3 places, the world at 6, the game at 24. The journal right after a finished visit shows a half-empty page with three “Still to find” tiles. Once everywhere is visited the gold card sends the child back to a world they just finished (“More to find · Tap Mars”), styled exactly like a brand-new world.
  5. A locked world says why only in words.Tapping a padlocked card shakes it for 0.4 s and shows “Visit The Moon first” (the article is capitalised mid-sentence) for 2.6 s. The world that unlocks it is not pictured, pointed at or highlighted; the gold stays on the suggested world.

Other changes: sound off, reload mid-visit, grown-ups

unclear

Nothing breaks, but sound off and a reload both remove the only cues the map has.

What changed
Sound off: Listen becomes a purple Words button and every spoken next step ("X is ready", "Now you can jump") is gone. Reload mid-visit: back on the Space map with no splash; the visit is dropped, found places stay in the journal. Grown-ups panel: the same adult page as the splash, reached by holding Journal for 2 s or ?grownups.
How a child can tell
Sound off looks almost identical. A reload simply shows the map again.
What they can do next
Carry on from the map.
How the next step is shown without reading
With sound off the screen has to carry everything, which makes states 8, 10, 14, 15, 16 and 22 worse. In code, a world unlocked by an arrival (markVisited runs on arrival) and then interrupted by a reload or crash is revealed silently on the next load: boot calls applyReveal() without newlyRevealed, so no “X is ready” at all. (The reload shot here re-seeds progress from the capture script, so it does not show that.)
